Until now there was one way to help in Pyr: pick a cell and proofread it until it is complete. Today there is a second. A Quest asks one question about a cell, and you answer it by marking what you find. You do not have to proofread the cell to do it.

The first is the **Cilia Quest**, on the Retina dataset. It comes from Celia, who describes it like this:

> We're on the hunt for primary cilia, important cellular appendages that receive and process environmental signals. Cilia serve as “antennas” that cells use to sense the chemistry of their environment. Each cell only has one primary cilium, will you be the one to find it?

## What you are looking for

Each cell in the Quest is a starburst cell from the retina. Somewhere on its cell body there may be a cilium, a thin appendage that projects from the cell. At its base sits the basal body, and close by is a second structure, the daughter centriole.

You mark three things, each in its own layer:

- **Cilium centerline**, a line drawn along the cilium.
- **Basal body**, one point.
- **Daughter centriole**, one point.

Some cells have no visible cilium. That is an answer too, and you may still be able to find the two centrioles inside the cell.

## How to do one

1. Open the **Cell Library** and click **Quests**. If you are on another dataset, press **Switch to Retina**.
2. Press **Claim** on an open cell. The view goes to it and the three layers open.
3. Find the cilium and mark it. For the centerline, hold **Ctrl** and click along the cilium, then press **Enter**. **Backspace** takes back the last point and **Esc** cancels the line.
4. Put the crosshairs inside the cell and press **Submit**. Choose a cilium status from the list, and add a note if something needs explaining. Under each status the form says when to use it.

You can hold three cells at a time. **Release** sends a cell back for someone else.

## Help along the way

Press **Instructions** at the top of the Quests tab. You will find:

- The lab's guide to locating and annotating cilia.
- A [training video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-l2-R-9vX6o&t=1525s).
- A reference that opens three real cells with the lab's own annotations. Each one shows a different status and says when that status applies.

Not sure about a cell? There is a status for that. Choose **Need help** and say why in the notes.

## Where your answer goes

When you submit, your status, your notes and a link to your view go to the lab, with your name and the date. Pyr celebrates with you and tells you how many annotations you placed on that cell and how many Quests you have completed. Your profile keeps the count.

## A new tool for everyone

The centerline needed a line with more than two points, so every annotation layer now has a polyline tool, on every dataset. It is the same polyline the lab's own viewer draws.

## Tell us how it goes

The Cilia Quest is the first Quest, and Quests are on Retina for now. If something is unclear or could work better, send it in with the feedback button. We read every one.
